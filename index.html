Eternal Letter from Naufal to Rity

To Rity, My Favorite Little Universe 🌌❤️

Dear Rity,

Okay, confession time.

Aku bisa bikin satu universe kecil buat kita, lengkap dengan bintang-bintang dan segala macam keajaibannya. Tapi anehnya, pas harus nulis sesuatu buat kamu, aku malah bingung harus mulai dari mana.

I mean, how do you put someone this special into words without sounding ridiculously cheesy?

But since this is our little universe, I guess being a little cheesy is allowed. Just this once. 😌

Sejak 7 Juli 2024, ada satu hal yang terus bertambah: jumlah hari yang kita lalui sejak cerita ini dimulai. Tapi buat aku, kamu bukan cuma tentang tanggal, angka, atau anniversary yang datang setiap tahun.

You're so much more than that.

Aku suka membayangkan masih banyak cerita yang bisa kita buat. Banyak hal kecil yang mungkin nanti jadi kenangan besar. Banyak alasan baru buat ketawa, saling menggoda, dan bilang, "Ih, kamu tuh ya..." sambil diam-diam tetap sayang.

And yes, sometimes I might annoy you a little. Consider it a complimentary feature of loving me. No refunds. 🤭

Tapi di balik semua candaan itu, ada sesuatu yang pengin aku bilang dengan serius.

Aku sayang kamu, Rity.

Not because everything has to be perfect. Not because every day has to feel magical. But because you're you, and that's already something incredibly special to me.

Aku nggak tahu persis seperti apa hari-hari kita nanti. There will always be things we can't predict. Tapi aku berharap kita terus punya ruang buat saling memahami, belajar, tumbuh, dan menemukan alasan baru untuk memilih satu sama lain.

And if someday you come back here—maybe on our anniversary, maybe on a completely random Tuesday—I hope this letter reminds you of something simple.

Di antara begitu banyak kemungkinan di dunia ini, aku bersyukur cerita kita bisa dimulai.

Bintang-bintang di website ini mungkin cuma animasi kecil. Tapi rasa sayang yang pengin aku sampaikan lewat semuanya? That's real.

So here's to us, to the little things, to the future, and to all the chapters we haven't written yet.

I love you, Rity.

And unfortunately for you, I plan on being your favorite little troublemaker for a very, very long time. ❤️

With all my love,

Naufal ⭐
